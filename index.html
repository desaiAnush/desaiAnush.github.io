PSU Apartment Price Fairness Tool

deployed

Tell it the rent, beds, distance to campus, and amenities — it tells you if the landlord is charging fair market or trying to pull one over on you. Trained on ~100 real State College listings I scraped from four sites, geocoded, and cleaned.

problem
Penn State students overpay on rent because there's no easy way to price-check a listing.
stack
Python · BeautifulSoup · SQLite · Pandas · scikit-learn · XGBoost · geopy · Streamlit · Plotly
result
Predicts fair rent within $200 on most listings (CV R² 0.46, MAE $198).

Used Car Price Predictor

notebook

Predicts what a used car should sell for based on its mileage, age, engine size, brand, and other specs. Gives you a number to negotiate against instead of just trusting the sticker price.

problem
Hard to tell if a used car listing is priced fairly without comparing it against a real dataset.
stack
Python · Pandas · scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter
result
Random Forest hits $641 MAE (R² 0.989) on a 50k-listing dataset, vs. $5,810 MAE for a linear regression baseline.
